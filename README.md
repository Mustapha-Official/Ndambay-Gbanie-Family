# Ndambay-Gbanie Family

## Rooted in love.

Ndambay-Gbanie Family is a digital family home created to preserve family memories, stories, values, photographs, and togetherness for present and future generations.

The website serves as a living family album where our roots, experiences, and important moments can be remembered and shared.

## 🌿 Purpose

The purpose of this website is to:

- Preserve family memories
- Share our family story
- Celebrate our roots
- Document family values
- Keep important photographs and moments
- Strengthen family connection
- Create a digital record for future generations

## ❤️ Family Values

The website is built around six important values:

- Love
- Respect
- Growth
- Unity
- Honesty
- Legacy

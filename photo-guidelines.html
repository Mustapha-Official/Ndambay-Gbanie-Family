Photo Guidelines

Photographs are more than images. They capture faces, moments, emotions, celebrations, and memories that may become even more valuable as time passes.

Ndambay-Gbanie Family uses photographs to help preserve our shared history and tell the story of our family across generations. These guidelines help us protect those memories while respecting the people who appear in them.

Share Photos With Meaning

Every photograph added to our family collection should have a purpose. Whether it captures a celebration, gathering, milestone, childhood memory, or everyday moment, it should contribute positively to our family's story.

We encourage family members to share photographs that are meaningful, respectful, and worth preserving for future generations.

Respect the People in the Photo

A photograph may belong to the person who took it, but the people appearing in it also deserve consideration and respect.

  • Think carefully before sharing someone else's photograph
  • Respect requests not to publish or display a photograph
  • Avoid sharing embarrassing or humiliating images
  • Do not use photographs to mock, shame, or disrespect anyone
  • Consider the privacy of children and vulnerable family members

Ask When Permission Matters

Whenever possible, family members should seek permission before publishing personal photographs, especially when the image is private, sensitive, or could make someone uncomfortable.

Respecting someone's wishes about their image is an important part of maintaining trust and dignity within the family.

Keep Photos Appropriate

Our family collection should remain a welcoming space for people of different ages and generations. Photographs should therefore be appropriate and respectful of the wider family community.

  • Avoid offensive or inappropriate content
  • Avoid photographs intended to embarrass another person
  • Do not share images that expose private situations
  • Consider how a photograph may be viewed by future generations
  • Choose images that represent family members with dignity

Preserve the Original Memory

Family photographs are part of our history. When possible, original images should be preserved to maintain the authenticity and quality of important memories.

Editing may be useful for improving clarity, brightness, or overall image quality. However, photographs should not be manipulated in a way that intentionally misrepresents people or important family events.

Help Us Tell the Story Behind the Photo

A photograph becomes even more meaningful when future generations know the story behind it. Whenever possible, include useful information about an important photograph.

  • The names of people appearing in the photograph
  • The approximate date or year it was taken
  • The location where the photograph was taken
  • The event or occasion being remembered
  • A short story or memory connected to the moment

Protect Children's Privacy

Extra care should always be taken when sharing photographs of children. Parents or guardians should have their wishes respected regarding how and where photographs of their children are displayed.

Images of children should never be shared in a way that could expose them to unnecessary risk or reveal sensitive personal information.

Do Not Misuse Family Photographs

Photographs shared through Ndambay-Gbanie Family are intended to preserve family memories and strengthen our connection. They should not be used for harmful, deceptive, or disrespectful purposes.

  • Do not use family photographs to impersonate another person
  • Do not use images for fraud, deception, or harmful activities
  • Do not alter images to intentionally damage someone's reputation
  • Do not publish private photographs without proper consideration
  • Do not use family memories for commercial purposes without permission

Requesting the Removal of a Photograph

We understand that circumstances and personal preferences can change over time. If a family member feels uncomfortable with a photograph appearing on the website, they are encouraged to raise the concern respectfully.

Requests for review, updates, or removal of photographs should be taken seriously and handled with respect for everyone involved.

Preserving Photos for Future Generations

Today's photographs may become tomorrow's history. A simple image that seems ordinary today could become a valuable memory for children, grandchildren, and generations yet to come.

By caring for our photographs and documenting the stories behind them, we help ensure that future generations will not only see familiar faces but also understand the lives and moments connected to them.

Our Promise

We will treat family photographs as part of our shared heritage. We will aim to preserve them with care, share them with respect, and protect the dignity of every person whose memory becomes part of our family story.

A photograph captures a moment, but the story behind it can live for generations.

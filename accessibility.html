Accessibility

At Ndambay-Gbanie Family, we believe that every family member should be able to access, explore, and enjoy the stories and memories shared on this website.

Our goal is to create a digital home that feels welcoming and easy to use for people of different ages, abilities, devices, and levels of technical experience.

Our Commitment

We are committed to making Ndambay-Gbanie Family as accessible and user-friendly as reasonably possible. We aim to design the website in a way that allows visitors to navigate content comfortably and understand the information being shared.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we will continue to improve the website as our family grows and technology changes.

Designed for Different Devices

Family members may visit this website using different devices. For this reason, we aim to make the website responsive and comfortable to use across a range of screen sizes.

  • Mobile phones
  • Tablets
  • Laptops
  • Desktop computers

The website layout may adjust depending on the device and screen size to provide a better browsing experience.

Clear and Readable Content

We aim to present information in a clear and understandable way. Family stories, guidelines, and other content should be easy to read without unnecessary complexity.

We also aim to use meaningful headings, readable text, and organized content to help visitors find the information they are looking for.

Navigation and Ease of Use

A family website should not be difficult to explore. We aim to provide simple navigation that helps visitors move between important sections of the website.

  • Clear navigation links
  • Organized website sections
  • Easy-to-understand labels
  • Responsive menus for smaller screens
  • Logical movement between pages and sections

Images and Visual Content

Family photographs are an important part of our story. Where possible, we aim to provide meaningful descriptions for images so that visitors using assistive technologies can better understand their purpose and content.

We also aim to avoid relying entirely on images to communicate important information whenever text can provide useful additional context.

Technology and Compatibility

We aim to make the website work properly with modern web browsers and commonly used devices. However, the appearance or functionality of some features may vary depending on browser versions, device capabilities, or internet connection quality.

For the best experience, we recommend using an updated web browser and a stable internet connection whenever possible.

Supporting Different Users

Our family includes people of different ages and levels of familiarity with technology. We believe that digital spaces should not exclude someone simply because they are less experienced with modern devices.

We therefore aim to keep the website simple, clear, and welcoming for both younger and older generations.

Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is not something that is completed once and forgotten. As the website grows, we may discover areas that can be improved.

We welcome constructive feedback that can help us make Ndambay-Gbanie Family easier and more comfortable for everyone to use.

Let Us Know If You Need Help

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, encounter a feature that does not work properly, or have suggestions for improving accessibility, please contact us using the information provided on the Contact page.

Your feedback can help us identify problems and improve the experience for other family members and visitors.

Our Promise

Ndambay-Gbanie Family was created to connect generations, preserve memories, and provide a welcoming place for our family's story. We will continue to make reasonable efforts to ensure that this digital home remains open, simple, and accessible to everyone.

Because every family member deserves a place in the story.

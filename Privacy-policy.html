Privacy Policy

Welcome to Ndambay-Gbanie Family. This Privacy Policy explains how we respect and protect the personal information, photographs, stories, and memories shared within our family website.

Ndambay-Gbanie Family was created as a digital home where family members can celebrate their history, preserve meaningful moments, and stay connected across generations.

Our Commitment to Privacy

We believe that family memories and personal information deserve to be treated with care and respect. This website is designed primarily for family connection, remembrance, and the preservation of our shared story.

We do not intend to collect or use family information for commercial purposes. Any information shared on this website should serve a meaningful purpose within our family community.

Information We Respect

Our family website may contain personal information intended to preserve our history and strengthen our connection.

  • Names and family relationships
  • Family photographs and videos
  • Stories, memories, and family history
  • Important family events and milestones
  • Contact information shared voluntarily

Family Photographs and Memories

Photographs and stories are an important part of our family's history. They help us remember the people, places, celebrations, and experiences that have shaped our journey.

We encourage everyone to treat family photographs and memories with respect. Content shared through this website should not be misused, manipulated, or publicly redistributed without proper consideration for the people involved.

Protecting Personal Information

We make reasonable efforts to maintain and protect the content shared on Ndambay-Gbanie Family. However, no website or internet service can guarantee complete security at all times.

Family members are encouraged to avoid sharing highly sensitive personal information publicly and to use good judgment when contributing content to the website or related family platforms.

Information We Encourage You Not to Share

For personal safety and privacy, highly sensitive information should not be published or shared publicly through this website.

  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Bank account or financial information
  • National identification numbers
  • Private security information
  • Highly sensitive personal documents
  • Information that could put a family member at risk

Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to external platforms and communication services, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or other services used by family members.

When visiting an external website, that platform may have its own privacy policy and practices. We encourage users to understand the privacy rules of external services when sharing personal information.

Children's Privacy

The privacy and safety of children should always be taken seriously. When sharing photographs or information involving children, family members should use good judgment and respect the wishes of parents or guardians.

Content involving children should never be shared in a way that could unnecessarily expose them to risk or compromise their privacy.

Respecting Personal Wishes

Every family member deserves to have their privacy respected. If someone feels uncomfortable with a photograph, story, name, or personal detail appearing on this website, their concern should be taken seriously.

Where appropriate, content may be reviewed, updated, or removed to respect the privacy and wishes of the individual involved.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

As Ndambay-Gbanie Family grows and changes over time, this Privacy Policy may occasionally be updated to reflect new features or changes in how family information is managed.

Any future updates will continue to reflect our commitment to privacy, respect, and responsible sharing.

Contact Us

If you have a question, concern, or request regarding privacy or personal information shared on this website, please contact the family using the contact information provided on the website.

Our Promise

Ndambay-Gbanie Family exists to bring generations closer and preserve the memories that matter most. We are committed to treating our family's stories, photographs, and personal information with care and respect.

Because memories may belong to the past, but respect belongs to every generation.

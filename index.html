What we stand against

Some lines are worth keeping.

Our family is not perfect, but we are intentional about protecting the kind of environment where people feel safe, valued and heard.

×

Disrespect

No insults, humiliation or deliberate disregard for another person's dignity.

×

Division

We do not nurture unnecessary conflict, favoritism or behavior that tears people apart.

×

Dishonesty

We reject manipulation and deception. Difficult truth is better than comfortable lies.

×

Neglect

We try not to disappear when someone needs encouragement, support or a helping hand.

×

Gossip

Private matters should be handled with maturity, discretion and direct conversation.

×

Giving up

We encourage resilience and remind one another that hard seasons can change.
